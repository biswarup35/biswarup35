## 👨‍💻 About Me

I operate at the intersection of high-throughput data engineering, audience intelligence, and modern go-to-market systems. With 4+ years of hands-on experience, I specialize in architecting real-time streaming pipelines, attribution engines, and high-performance analytical platforms that turn massive ad event streams into actionable revenue and growth.

- 🚀 **Founder @ [Maagnetix.com](https://maagnetix.com)** — Building next-generation digital infrastructure, content/CMS systems, and growth engineering tools.
- 🎯 **GTM Engineer & Ad Tech Specialist** — Deep domain expertise in ad event streaming (impressions, clicks, conversions, attribution), advertiser reporting dashboards, and audience profiling.
- ⚡ **Real-Time Data & OLAP** — Designing low-latency analytical engines with **ClickHouse**, **Apache Kafka**, **Snowflake**, **Databricks**, and **dbt**.

## 🎯 What I Do & Specialize In

### 1. Ad Tech & GTM Engineering
- **Ad Event Streaming & Ingestion**: Processing high-velocity impressions, clicks, conversions, and attribution events via Kafka into analytics-ready datasets.
- **Advertiser Analytics & Reporting**: Building real-time reporting platforms and audience segmentation models for campaign performance and ROAS optimization.
- **Technical GTM Workflows**: Connecting product telemetry, data warehousing, and marketing automation to accelerate go-to-market execution.

### 2. High-Performance Data & Real-Time OLAP
- **Sub-Second Analytics**: Designing optimized table engines, partition strategies, and materialized views in **ClickHouse** and **Snowflake** for sub-second query latency.
- **Stream & Batch Pipelines**: Orchestrating ETL/ELT pipelines using **Apache Spark**, **Kafka**, and **dbt** with incremental builds for optimal data mart efficiency.
- **Data Modeling & Governance**: Structuring robust schemas and data marts for dimensional analysis, audience profiling, and AI/GenAI analytics copilots.

### 3. Full-Stack Product Engineering
- Building scalable microservices and APIs with **Node.js**, **Fastify**, **Express**, and **GraphQL**.
- Designing responsive, high-performance web applications using **React.js**, **Next.js**, and **TypeScript**.
- Containerized deployments and CI/CD automation with **Docker**, **GitHub Actions**, and **Linux**.
